## About the Speaker
Soroor is currently completing her PhD in Machine Learning and Computational Biology at Helmholtz Munich, Germany. Her research focuses on generalisation, domain shift, and the transferability of representations in biology, using methods from representation learning, causality, and statistical machine learning, with applications in cancer research and predictive modelling. She is currently working on large-scale perturbation response modelling that connects biological mechanisms to phenotypes.

Soroor is also a co-founder and former co-organiser (2017–2019) of R-Ladies Melbourne, and has previously worked and studied at the Walter and Eliza Hall Institute (WEHI) and the University of Melbourne.
